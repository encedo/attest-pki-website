Encedo Limited · certificate authorities

Every HEM carries a certificate.
Check it here.

Each Encedo HEM holds a key pair generated inside the device and a certificate for it, issued by the Encedo Custody CA for a HEM PPA or by the Encedo EPA Custody CA for a HEM EPA. This page publishes both chains up to the one root, with its revocation lists, so that any device can be checked against it with stock tools and nothing taken on our word.

Certificate authorities

Four authorities, one root.

Each certificate is published in DER (.cer, the form named in the certificates' own AIA fields) and in PEM. Compare the SHA-256 fingerprint with the file you downloaded before you install a root.

Revocation

Revocation lists

One list per authority, in DER, at the address named in the certificates' CRL distribution points. The lists are also served over plain HTTP, because some clients fetch them no other way; their signature, not the transport, is what protects them.

Verify

Check a device yourself.

Everything below runs with OpenSSL and nothing from us but the files on this page. The same files, with their history, are in the public repository.

1 · the files

Download the files and check them against SHA256SUMS.

$ sha256sum -c SHA256SUMSattestation-ca.cer: OK…
2 · the root

Compare the root's fingerprint with the one printed above.

$ openssl x509 -in root-ca.pem -noout -fingerprint -sha256sha256 Fingerprint=B2:8F:4E:E4:CF:26:02:82:…
3 · the device

Verify the device certificate against its chain: intermediates-chain.pem for a HEM PPA, epa-intermediates-chain.pem for a HEM EPA.

$ openssl verify -CAfile root-ca.pem -untrusted intermediates-chain.pem device.pemdevice.pem: OK
4 · revocation

Check the chain with every revocation list applied; the same commands serve both models.

$ cat root-ca.pem intermediates-chain.pem epa-custody-ca.pem > chain.pem$ for c in root attestation custody epa-custody; do openssl crl -inform DER -in $c-ca.crl; done > crls.pem$ openssl verify -crl_check_all -CAfile chain.pem -CRLfile crls.pem device.pemdevice.pem: OK
